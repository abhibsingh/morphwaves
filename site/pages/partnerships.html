Why talk to us

Three things we bring to a collaboration at this stage.

Focused engineering attention

As an early venture, a partner's problem gets direct founder level engineering time rather than a queue.

Automation and reliability depth

Test automation, observability and API integration are core skills, applied here to ground segment systems.

Willingness to start small

A feasibility study or a narrow pilot on one station is a perfectly good first step for us.

Who we want to work with

What a collaboration could look like for each kind of organisation.

  • Satellite operators

    Improve pass reliability and data completeness with monitoring and validation around your existing ground network.

  • Ground station providers

    Automate pre pass readiness checks, station health reporting and customer facing visibility.

  • Space technology startups

    Share integration work, combine complementary products, or co develop tooling neither team should build alone.

  • Research institutions

    Collaborate on anomaly detection, scheduling optimisation and student or university mission operations.

  • Hardware manufacturers

    Build and test software integrations for antennas, modems and RF equipment, with automated verification.

  • Systems integrators

    Bring us in for the software, automation and testing layer of a ground segment you are delivering.

  • Potential pilot customers

    Shape GroundOps as a design partner, starting with one station and one well defined problem.

What happens after you get in touch

A simple path, with no commitment until both sides see value.

  1. Introductory call

    Thirty minutes on your operations, your systems and where reliability or effort hurts most.

  2. Scoping note

    A short written summary of a possible collaboration, its risks and what success would look like.

  3. Pilot or study

    If it makes sense to both of us, a time boxed pilot or feasibility study with clear outputs.
