About Morphwaves

Building Reliable Technology for an Increasingly Connected Space Economy.

Morphwaves is an early stage venture working on the part of the space industry that rarely makes headlines and always decides outcomes: operations on the ground.

Vision

We want ground operations to be as dependable and as well instrumented as the spacecraft they serve.

As constellations grow and more organisations put payloads in orbit, the number of contacts each ground network must handle rises quickly. Scheduling, monitoring and validation that once worked by hand will not scale with that demand. We see a lasting need for software that keeps operators informed, catches faults early and connects cleanly to the systems they already trust.

Why ground infrastructure matters

A satellite is only as useful as its link to the ground. Every image, measurement and command passes through a ground station during a pass that may last only minutes. If a pass is missed, a feed goes stale or a configuration drifts, data is lost and mission time is wasted.

Ground segments are also complex. Antennas, RF chains, modems, scheduling tools and mission control software often come from different vendors and different decades. The weak points are usually at the seams between them, which is where careful integration and continuous verification make the largest difference.

An engineering led approach

We would rather show a working integration than describe a platform. These principles shape how we build.

  • Start from the operator's day

    Design around the real sequence of a pass: planning, acquisition, tracking, data handling and review.

  • Verify everything that moves

    Automated tests, validation and monitoring around every integration point, run continuously rather than before a launch only.

  • Fit existing systems

    Integrate through documented APIs and adapters instead of asking stations to replace equipment that already works.

  • Use AI where it earns its place

    Apply machine learning to anomaly analysis and diagnostics where it gives operators clearer signals, and keep humans in control of decisions.

  • Be clear about maturity

    State plainly what is available, what is in development and what is a long term ambition.

A software first business strategy

Building ground stations requires capital, spectrum coordination and regulatory authorisation. Software does not need any of those to start delivering value, and it teaches us how stations really behave under load.

So we are starting with ground segment software and integration engineering. With pilot partners, we plan to prove that work against live operations. Only then do we intend to explore commercial ground infrastructure and Ground Station as a Service, together with authorised operators.

Contributing to India's space ecosystem

India's space sector has opened to private participation, with IN-SPACe authorising and supporting non government entities and a growing number of startups building launch vehicles, satellites and ground systems.

That growth creates demand for dependable ground operations at every scale, from university missions to commercial constellations. Morphwaves wants to contribute practical, well tested software to that ecosystem, and to work with Indian operators, manufacturers and research institutions as it develops.
