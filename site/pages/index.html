What happens on the ground decides what a mission gets from orbit.

Every satellite depends on ground stations to schedule contacts, receive telemetry, send commands and confirm that each pass went as planned. That work is still held together by manual checks, scattered tools and late alerts.

Morphwaves is developing software driven approaches to satellite ground operations, with a focus on monitoring, integration and automation. We start with software because it is where reliability can improve fastest, and we intend to grow toward ground infrastructure with the right operators and partners.

Where we are today. Morphwaves is early stage. We do not operate ground stations or satellites, and we hold no regulatory authorisations. We are building software concepts and looking for operators and partners to shape them with.

How we engineer

Ground systems fail in quiet ways: a missed pass, a stale feed, a configuration that drifted. Our engineering habits are built to catch exactly that.

Verified by default

Automated tests and validation around every integration, so a change to one system cannot quietly break another.

Observable end to end

Logs, metrics and health signals designed in from the start, so operators can see what a station is doing and why.

Integration through APIs

Clean, documented interfaces that fit the systems a station already runs, rather than asking teams to replace them.
