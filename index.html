MorphWave — Every Surface Has A Voice

MorphWave is an acoustic transducer that turns any rigid surface into a speaker. The device itself is almost silent; attached to wood, glass, metal, cardboard or a wall, the surface becomes the sound. This page tells that story: a magnetic core appears, a copper voice coil winds itself into being, precision components assemble, the housing closes, and the finished device brings tables, windows and walls to life with music. Built to make anything speak. MorphWave — redefining the future of sound.
